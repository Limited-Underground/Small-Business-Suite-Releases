# Feedback and bug reports

Limited Underground Business welcomes voluntary bug reports and suggestions.
There is no formal tester program, enrollment process, or requirement to provide
feedback. External tester participation is not a prerequisite for release.
Internal verification and the owner's release approval remain required.

## Send ordinary feedback

[Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/Limited-Underground/Small-Business-Suite-Releases/issues/new)
for a non-sensitive bug report or suggestion. A GitHub account is required.
Include the application version, Windows version, steps to reproduce, what you
expected, and what actually happened. Use fictional data in examples. Reports
are welcome but do not promise a response time, fix, or new feature.

## Keep private information private

GitHub issues are public. Do not attach business databases, backups, raw logs,
customer or employee information, payment details, credentials, private reports,
or screenshots containing sensitive information. Do not upload a support bundle
without reviewing and removing private information first.

For a suspected security vulnerability, use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Limited-Underground/Small-Business-Suite-Releases/security/advisories/new),
not a public issue. See [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Download status

See the [README](README.md) for the currently published package and its limits.
The Free distribution is not yet publicly released. The historical seven-day
trial remains a trial; voluntary feedback does not change its expiration rules.
